# Reference photos

Photos of Vincent to check pixel-Vincent against (`tools/models/characters.py`, colours in
`tools/models/palette.py`). Not published on the site.

- `vincent-terrace.png`: at a table outside a mountain hut, rust-red v-neck tee.
- `vincent-hiking.png`: on a forest hike, navy v-neck tee, navy cap worn backwards,
  daypack, navy shorts, dark sports watch.

What stays the same in both: slim build, tanned skin, short brown hair, short beard that goes
grey at the chin, big open grin, sunglasses hooked in the collar of a v-neck tee.

- `vincent-and-zola.png`: selfie at home with Zola, his late black cat, draped over his
  shoulder. Zola is all black with white front paws and green eyes. This one shows Vincent
  with longer, messier hair and a fuller beard, wearing over-ear headphones.
